About A small game, made carefully

Built for the
love of the game.

Tic Tac Toe, The Unbeatable Edition is a portfolio piece about the details: perfect game logic, tactile interaction, thoughtful accessibility, and a theme for every mood.

01

Unbeatable by design

The AI uses exhaustive minimax search with alpha-beta pruning. It never loses. Against perfect play, the best result available to you is a draw.

02

Two ways to play

Challenge the machine, or pass the device to a friend in local two-player mode. Choose X or O, take back a move, or ask for a hint.

03

Twelve atmospheres

From bare gallery paper to late-night arcade neon, every palette is hand-tuned for contrast, clarity, and a distinct point of view.

04

Care in the details

Scores persist on your device, sound is generated in the browser, motion respects your system preferences, and every control works from the keyboard.
